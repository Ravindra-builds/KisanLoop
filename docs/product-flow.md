# KisanLoop Product Flow & The Action Loop

```text
       DETECT (IMD Radar, Sensors, Sentinel-2 NDVI)
          ↓
       UNDERSTAND (Farm State Engine, Weather + Soil Context)
          ↓
       RECOMMEND (Actionable Advisory with Confidence & Deadline)
          ↓
       CHECK FEASIBILITY (Feasibility Engine: Cost, Availability, Labor)
          ↓
       ACT (Done vs I Couldn't Do This)
          ↓
       VERIFY (Outcome Engine, Expert Review, KVK Verification)
          ↓
       LEARN (Continuous Regional Intelligence & Better Next Action)
```

---

## 1. Hero Journey: Ravi Kumar (Namkum, Ranchi)
1. **Intelligence Arrival**: Radar detects 85% probability of 42mm rainfall tomorrow. Soil moisture sensor indicates root-zone moisture is at 68.5% (adequate).
2. **Context Engine Evaluation**: Adding more water today would lead to root asphyxiation, nutrient leaching, and fuel waste.
3. **Recommendation Formulation**: "Hold Irrigation Today (आज सिंचाई न करें)".
4. **Feasibility Validation**: Action requires zero capital and conserves resources (Passes 100%).
5. **Action Presentation**: Large, accessible buttons on mobile UI with one-touch audio reading in Hindi.
6. **Farmer Execution**: Ravi Kumar confirms action with one tap ("कर लिया / Done").
7. **Traceability**: All actions and evidence logged in the **Recommendation Passport**.
8. **Outcome Measurement**: Avoided waterlogging during the storm; preserved root health; saved 12,000L groundwater and ₹650 pumping cost.
